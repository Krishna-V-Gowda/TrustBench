# Contributing

A contribution should improve executable evidence rather than add ungrounded claims.

For a new check, include:

1. the invariant or named statistical definition;
2. a minimal fixture or generator;
3. an independent oracle or metamorphic property;
4. explicit tolerance and missing-value semantics;
5. a stable finding identifier;
6. a regression test for the adapter and report;
7. a limitation statement when the conclusion is narrower than the title.

Do not copy subject algorithms into an oracle. Do not turn expected disagreements into passing tests merely to make CI green. Update a subject lock only after reviewing the source change and regenerating evidence.
